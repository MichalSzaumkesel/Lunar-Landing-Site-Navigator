# Demo script and 30-second video (scope B)

Draft, data-driven. Every number is read from the screen at the moment of the demo, never recalled from memory. The app labels synthetic data with a badge; say so when it is visible. Words to use: "Sun visible", "Earth in view". Never claim power, energy or link availability.

Before the demo: open the app at `/?tour=0` (no guided tour), full screen, default scenario (south-pole site, window start 2027-08-30; re-tune the start date in `config/presets.ts` when real data arrives so that the Sun flips several times), sound off, Advanced mode. Keep a second tab on the Simple mode as a backup.

### If something fails

- **WebGL or projector GPU problem**: stay on the Polar and Panorama views; everything else is SVG and canvas.
- **No network**: sites visited once are cached in the page; open the three demo sites before the demo. Do not say "fully offline" unless the demo sites were opened first.
- **Small display**: the board stacks to one column below 900 px; Space plays and pauses, arrows step an hour.
- **Anything odd on screen**: reload with `/?tour=0`; the state is in the link.

### Likely questions (answers come from `docs/method.md` of scope A; until it exists say "see the method page")

1. How is the horizon computed? Elevation model rays, curvature included (`CLAUDE.md` physics notes).
2. Is the Sun a point? No: angular radius is used and the visible disc fraction is reported next to the Sun-visible state.
3. Can you guarantee power or a link? No. The app shows geometric visibility only.
