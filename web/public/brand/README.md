# Lunar Landing Site Navigator — Brand Assets & Style Guide

This directory contains the official brand assets, icon sets, lockups, and vector masters for Lunar Landing Site Navigator.

## Typography & License

The wordmark outlines and social image tagline curves are derived from the [Inter](https://github.com/rsms/inter) typeface.

- **Font**: Inter (designed by Rasmus Andersson and contributors)
- **License**: SIL Open Font License 1.1 ([http://scripts.sil.org/OFL](http://scripts.sil.org/OFL))
- **License Text**: See [`FONT-LICENSE.txt`](file:///C:/Dev/spaceapps-rzeszow-2026/web/public/brand/FONT-LICENSE.txt) for the complete license terms.

All SVG files contain real vector path curves (`<path>`) and no `<text>` elements, guaranteeing identical rendering across every operating system, device, and browser without dependency on local system fonts.

## Clear-Space & Minimum-Size Rules

1. **Clear Space**: The minimum clear space around the badge mark and lockups equals the height of the Sun disc (approximately 32% of the mark diameter). Keep this zone free of other typography, illustrations, or competing UI elements.
2. **Minimum Size**:
   - `mark.svg`: Minimum 24 px width/height.
   - `mark-small.svg`: Minimum 16 px width/height (used for 16 px and 32 px favicons with simplified ridge lines and Earth disc omitted for legibility).
   - Horizontal lockups: Minimum height 24 px.
   - Vertical lockups: Minimum height 60 px.

---

## Usage Guidelines ("Do Not" Rules)

- **Do not stretch or distort**: Always maintain original 1:1 aspect ratios for marks and standard proportions for lockups.
- **Do not recolor arbitrarily**: Use only the designated on-dark, on-light, mono-black, or mono-white brand colorways.
- **Do not rotate or skew**: The lunar horizon and solar alignment must remain horizontally oriented.
- **Do not add drop shadows, glows, or gradients**: The mark is flat and geometric.
- **Do not rearrange elements**: Do not move the Sun, Earth, or horizon silhouettes within the mark badge.
