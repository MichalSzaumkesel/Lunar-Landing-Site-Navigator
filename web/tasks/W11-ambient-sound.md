# Task W11: ambient sound that follows Sun and Earth visibility (replaces the beeps as the default sound)

Write the files first, then test (see `_common.md`; follow `tasks/UI-guidelines.md`, `RULES-quality.md`). Today sound is a set of short beep cues on events (`src/lib/sound.ts`, `src/components/sound/**`, `useEventSounds`). The user wants continuous, calm sound instead: a tone that swells while the Sun is visible and fades away to silence when it is not, and a second, different tone for the Earth that does the same.

## Writable paths

`src/lib/sound*.ts` (and tests), `src/components/sound/**`, `src/app/Tools.tsx` (only the sound control). Nothing else. Another worker may be editing `src/app/TopBar.tsx`, `src/components/demo/**`, `src/components/timeline/**` and `src/viz3d/**`; do not touch them.

## Behaviour

1. **Modes**: the existing sound toggle becomes a small three-state control, "Off / Ambient / Cues" (radiogroup named "Sound", no visible label). Default **Off**. "Ambient" is what a user gets when they switch sound on; "Cues" keeps the old beeps unchanged. Persist the choice in `localStorage` (wrap every access in try/catch; the control must work without storage). Audio starts only after the user's click (autoplay policy: create or resume the `AudioContext` inside the click handler).
2. **Sun voice** (follows the ACTIVE scenario at the shared time cursor): a warm low pad built from 3 slightly detuned oscillators (sine and triangle, roughly A2, E3, A3), through a low-pass filter. Loudness follows the visible fraction of the Sun disc (0 to 1; the same function that drives the sky plot readout "Disc %"; find and reuse it, do not recompute the physics): gain = smooth curve of the fraction, so a half-cut Sun is clearly quieter and slowly shimmers (slow tremolo, depth growing as the fraction drops below 1, to hint "partly hidden"), and at fraction 0 the voice fades to true silence. The filter opens a little with the Sun elevation (brighter when higher).
3. **Earth voice**: a different timbre so the two are distinguishable with eyes closed: higher register and airy (two sines a perfect fifth apart around E4 and B4, a little band-passed noise "breath", slower attack). Loudness follows the visible fraction of the Earth disc the same way; brightness follows the Earth lit fraction (a crescent is darker than a full Earth). Silent when the Earth is not in view.
4. **Both together** sound consonant (a chord), neither visible is silence. Stereo pan follows azimuth (`pan = sin(azimuth)`, north centre, east right) with a `StereoPannerNode`.
5. **Smoothness**: never set gain instantly; use `setTargetAtTime` with a time constant of about 0.35 s so there are no clicks, also when the user scrubs or Play runs at 1 d/s or faster (then the sound should become a slow swell, not flutter: limit parameter updates to about 15 per second and smooth). Fade out over about 0.6 s when the mode is switched off, then close or suspend the context.
6. **Safety and manners**: master gain around -20 dBFS through a `DynamicsCompressorNode` as a soft limiter; a small volume slider (default 40%) next to the mode control; suspend when the tab is hidden (`visibilitychange`) and resume when visible if the mode is still Ambient; dispose all nodes on unmount and when the mode changes (stop oscillators, disconnect); no sound while `prefers-reduced-motion`-independent (this is audio, so ignore that setting) but never start by itself. Drive updates from a store subscription outside React rendering (no re-render per update).
7. **Accessibility and copy**: the control is a radiogroup with `aria-label` "Sound"; a one-line hint under or beside it when Ambient is selected: "Sun and Earth fade in and out with visibility." Sound is supplementary: nothing may depend on it. Wording rule: only "Sun visible" and "Earth in view"; never power, energy, signal or link claims.
8. **Tests**: pure helpers with unit tests (fraction to gain curve, elevation to filter cutoff, Earth lit fraction to brightness, azimuth to pan including wrap, update rate limiter, mode persistence with a throwing `localStorage`); a mocked `AudioContext` test that the graph is created on enable, parameters ramp (not jump) on a change, and everything is stopped and disconnected on disable and unmount. Do not test the sound itself.

## Acceptance

`npm run typecheck`, `npm run lint`, `npm test` exit 0 (if tests in files owned by others fail because of their in-progress edits, say so and run only your paths). Report per item: done or why not, test counts. Say plainly that the sound itself could not be listened to.
